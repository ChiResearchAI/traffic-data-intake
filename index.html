A sixty-second segment processed by ChiResearch in October 2026 on licensed source footage, 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps. This is an internal validation run, not a client engagement and not a Charlotte location. It is shown because the figures below are measured output, and because the error it surfaced is the kind of error verification exists to catch.

The annotated video is not carried on this host. The measured results below are unchanged.

What verification caught. The engine placed the three person detections inside its own “Total Vehicles” figure and reported them again as non-motorized, giving 221 where the true vehicle count is 218. Its heavy-vehicle share divided 24 heavy vehicles by the inflated 221 for 10.9 percent; over the true 218 the figure is 11.0 percent — the ratio survives the error the volume does not. A second pass with the detection zones redrawn on identical footage moved northbound from 107 to 110 and southbound from 107 to 108, and added three northbound and one southbound that hand review had not flagged.

About the intake tooling below. Operational: count-type selection, Form 7 cost estimate, count-number assignment, the 72-hour processing clock, and the Appendix A §2.H footage conformance check with SHA-256 file hashing — the conformance check runs in your browser on a local file, so a 30 GB recording is validated without being uploaded anywhere. Specified and not yet built: authenticated resumable multi-gigabyte transfer into immutable storage. Until that is in service, video is received by online file transfer service (Appendix A §15.C.iv and §16.E.iii name that medium as preferred) and task-order coordination runs by email, which Addendum 1, Item 6 states is acceptable.
